# Uber — guild announcement

**Uber coordinates Warlock summons and Mage portals.** Install the Uber folder and type **/uber**.

Browse reviewed providers, check prices and hours, request a dungeon/camp, add supplies/notes, and follow setup status and map location. Gray cards are offline; schedule requests up to 30 days ahead. Offline requests stay on your character until delivered and accepted — they are not guaranteed bookings.

**Creator/reviewer: Bîfrost Titan.** Mage/Warlock applicants use Recruitment at a mailbox, review the prepared application and click WoW's Send button. Providers need manual review. The creator manages approval, rates, hours and removal/bans.

Mage portals include a configured 22s minimum per portal cast; additional fees, priority and tips are itemized before acceptance. Warlock base service can be free. Mail payments remain player-controlled. The unaccented **Bifrost Titan** is the separate house-payment recipient. Normal realm/faction/mail restrictions apply.

Receipts & delivery timeline distinguishes native mailbox observations, provider casts and customer arrival reports. Single-person delivery can complete automatically after party/cast/matching arrival checks; Warlocks also match the summon offer. Reports are client evidence, not guaranteed proof of delivery or a refund service.

All addon packets run without opening chat or interrupting movement. No C++ helper is required. Version 0.4.0-beta.1 remains a beta: report issues with the exact client build and steps. Live multiplayer/spell/mail checks are still in progress. The optional Guild LFG button opens TranquilityLFG if installed.

The fantasy logo is AI-generated branding, not gameplay. Independent community addon, not affiliated with Uber Technologies, Blizzard or CurseForge.
