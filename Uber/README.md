# Uber

One addon for Warlock summons, Mage portals, customers and reviewed providers. Open **/uber**. Targets Forever Interface 16001; standalone, MIT, no required addon or executable dependency.

## Start here

- Drivers: browse approved providers. Creator **Bîfrost Titan** stays listed offline. Gray means offline/unconfirmed, not ready service.
- Book a summon: choose a provider, destination, group size, supplies and notes. Configure price/priority/tip options; Mage orders have a 22s minimum per portal cast.
- Schedule ahead: choose a local date/time up to 30 days ahead. Offline reservations remain local until the provider returns; receipt and manual acceptance are separate steps.
- Track & map: watch service status, chosen ETA and fresh provider location. Readiness whispers are clicked actions.
- Driver desk: open service explicitly after login/reload, inspect requests and quotes, accept reservations or start setup, and update camp status.
- Recruitment: prepare a native-mail application to accented **Bîfrost Titan**, then review and click Send yourself. The creator reviews class, readiness and conduct manually.
- Creator control room: add/approve/unban, remove or ban named providers; manage base/priority prices and regular hours including timezone. Native founder sender and increasing policy revisions authorize changes. Offline clients must refresh for updates.
- Receipts & delivery timeline: inspect timestamped cast, travel, native mailbox and reported evidence; filter by character or order.

## Delivery rules

A successful portal/summon cast, including an instant cast, is recorded while provider service is open. Delivery additionally requires a linked rider in the party, a matching customer transition within 180 seconds and the expected arrival map/position. Warlocks also require a native summon offer naming the provider. Single-person, single-rider service can complete automatically; group bookings stay open until all riders are accounted for. Unsupported/restricted observations remain unconfirmed. Client reports are not server-certified proof and do not reveal everyone who used an arbitrary Mage portal.

## Payments

Mages keep the entire configured 22s portal minimum. Additional service/priority charges split 20% house / 80% provider; tips belong entirely to the provider. Quotes name recipients and amounts. The house defaults to unaccented **Bifrost Titan** on the current realm, distinct from the accented creator identity. Native mail restrictions apply; no automatic cross-realm transfers.

Uber prepares recipient/reference text only; the player enters and sends money through WoW. Mail-send notices are not receipt proof. Matching native letters are observed only in the local character's open mailbox. Remote evidence reports wait locally while the creator is offline. No escrow or guaranteed refunds.

## Communication and privacy

Native addon packets do not open chat, paste text or intercept movement/typing. Visible readiness/guild requests are clicked native whispers. No automatic casting, movement, recruitment loop or C++ helper is included. Character names, order notes, schedules, reviews, prices and service evidence are saved locally with bounded retention. Avoid sensitive notes. Ordinary logout/reload persists records; crashes may lose recent changes. Editable addons are not tamper-proof financial ledgers or account-authentication servers.

## Release checks

0.4.0-beta.1 is a beta candidate. Automated simulation is supplemented by required live mailbox, multiplayer, party/cast/arrival and UI checks. The commission model still requires platform-policy clarification. No claim of public acceptance, complete spell coverage, server-certified delivery or permanent global creator ownership is made. The addon is independent of Uber Technologies, Blizzard and CurseForge.

## Network activity and shared sessions

Open **Network & open sessions** in `/uber` for available summoner/portal-keeper counts, occupied drivers, fresh public-user observations and active camp destinations. Join an existing Ready/Setting up camp through the normal booking flow; native invitations, acceptance, payment and delivery still apply. Full reported camps disable joining. The green Connected indicator requires fresh peer traffic; discovery alone is not a successful connection.

The public statistics checkbox excludes your heartbeat from public counts. Its adjacent disclosure explains that your character identity, public/opt-out flag and recent activity still go privately to the fixed creator for admin census. Providers remain listed while offering service, and normal discovery/orders still expose their necessary sender identities to the involved providers. Only the creator can open the private census, including public opt-outs. No hidden-user roster is sent to other clients. Presence stays in memory, expires after 120 seconds and is not a permanent player history. Driver state is fresh for up to 90 seconds.

These are network observations, not exact realm population or a central-server census. Overlapping provider user counts use a conservative maximum rather than being added together. The creator must be online to receive private reports; clients must run the updated addon, and faction/realm/native transport limits still apply. Offline or opt-out clients are not magically discoverable. Heartbeats are paced, fan-out bounded and orders/receipts take priority over background packets.

An approved, service-active warlock who leads a native party requests raid conversion automatically outside combat. WoW permissions/confirmation restrictions remain respected. No automatic conversion back to party, disband, invitation acceptance or bypass is added; the provider handles those decisions.
